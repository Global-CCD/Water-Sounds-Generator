About Water Sounds Generator

This single-page web app lets you explore, mix, and visualize a wide variety of water sounds. From the calming sound of ocean waves to the mysterious echoes of underwater environments, this app is perfect for relaxation, focus, or creative inspiration.

All sounds are generated or played using the Web Audio API, ensuring a lightweight and responsive experience.

Features

  • ✅ Soundboard: Play individual water sounds with a single click.
  • ✅ Ambient Mixer: Mix multiple sounds to create custom ambiances.
  • ✅ Visualizer: Real-time audio visualization that reacts to the sounds.
  • ✅ Responsive Design: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
  • ✅ No Dependencies: Built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

How to Use

  1. Soundboard: Click on any sound card to play or stop the sound.
  2. Ambient Mixer: Adjust the volume sliders and toggle sounds on/off to create your mix.
  3. Visualizer: Play sounds and watch the canvas react in real-time.
